Method & caveats

  • Pickens missing changes Dallas' target shares; the market lines already reflect it, but the season averages shown do not.
  • Scenario probabilities come from the market (Pinnacle no-vig ML and spread where quoted, margin σ = 13.5), not the app's models, which trail the closing line.
  • Player projections start from each prop's market median and apply rule-of-thumb game-script multipliers (the leader runs more, the trailer throws more). They are not fitted to data, so read them as directional.
  • Scenario legs are conditional bets: they only make sense if you think that script is likelier than the market says. Unconditionally they are no better than the market price; the measured edges (Cowboys −4.5 at NoVig and Josh Downs Over 4.5 receptions) are on the main analysis.
