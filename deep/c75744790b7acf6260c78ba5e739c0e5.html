If the Ravens dominate

BAL 30–11 JAX 4% chance · avg margin 18.9

The upset path doesn't run through Fagnano's arm. It needs the rain, Baltimore's front and turnovers. Baltimore have been solid against the run (−0.064 EPA per carry), so Trey Hendrickson (#91) and Roquan Smith (#0) can make Jacksonville one-dimensional in poor weather, and a thin backfield helps if Tuten sits. Two takeaways or a special-teams score give a limited offense short fields, Henry finishes (51% of Baltimore's red-zone chances and 9 of their 20 TDs), and he then wears down a lead in the second half. Fagnano may only need a few safe throws to Zay Flowers (#4) and Mark Andrews (#89). Read this as an outlier: see the market's chance above.

What has to happen

  • Plus-two turnovers or better, ideally with a defensive or special-teams TD.
  • Henry past 20 carries, which only happens if Baltimore lead or stay level into the second half.
  • Lawrence pressured into mistakes on wet footing.
  • Fagnano avoids the giveaway that hurt at Buffalo.

Method & caveats

  • Inactives here are projected. Tuten decides who the Jacksonville RB rows apply to: if he sits, move his work to Rodriguez, and most books have no main rush line for either back yet.
  • Scenario probabilities come from the market (Pinnacle no-vig ML and spread, margin σ = 13.5), not the app's models, which trail the closing line.
  • Player projections start from each prop's market median and apply rule-of-thumb game-script multipliers (the leader runs more, the trailer throws more). They are not fitted to data, so read them as directional. They also don't know about the rain.
  • Scenario legs are conditional bets: they only make sense if you think that script is likelier than the market says. Unconditionally they are no better than the market price, and the measured edges are on the main analysis.
  • Both blowout scripts project about 42 points, a little over the 39.5 total, because the rule of thumb adds ~2 points to blowouts. The Under pick on the main analysis is a price edge for the whole range of outcomes, so don't pair it with a two-touchdown Jacksonville bet without noticing that tension.
  • Fagnano's numbers rest on one game, and the Baltimore usage rates mostly come from games with Jackson or Huntley.
