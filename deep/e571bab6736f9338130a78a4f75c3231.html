New Orleans Saints win by 10+

If the Saints dominate

NO 32–13 CLE 34% chance · avg margin 18.5

New Orleans win big if Tyler Shough (#6), at 39 attempts a game, feeds Chris Olave (#12) and debuting Jordyn Tyson (#0) while Sanders (#2) struggles. Sanders has barely played this season, and Cleveland's offense was already weak: −0.079 EPA per play, and only 47% of red-zone trips ending in TDs. The Saints have converted 71% in the red zone, so an early lead could get out of reach quickly. Cleveland are also without center Parker Brailsford (#52), which is a hard change for a first-time starter this year.

What has to happen

  • Olave over 90 yards with Tyson drawing some coverage.
  • Sanders taking sacks behind a new center.
  • Saints finishing drives with TDs, not field goals.
  • Cleveland stuck at its 47% red-zone TD rate.

If the Browns dominate

CLE 31–14 NO 16% chance · avg margin 16.6

Cleveland win big through defense and the run. The Browns are the better defense here (−0.017 EPA per play against +0.075), and Shough's passing volume gives them chances for turnovers. New Orleans are soft against the run (+0.071 per carry), which suits Quinshon Judkins (#10), though he has averaged only 49 rushing yards a game. What has to happen

  • Judkins above his season rushing average against a soft run defense.
  • Sanders completing short throws to Fannin and KC Concepcion (#1).
  • Saints held to field goals despite a 71% red-zone rate.

Method & caveats

  • Sanders has almost no regular-season sample this year, so his prop lines rest on little data. Tyson (#0) has no sample at all.
  • Scenario probabilities come from the market (Pinnacle no-vig ML and spread where quoted, margin σ = 13.5), not the app's models, which trail the closing line.
  • Player projections start from each prop's market median and apply rule-of-thumb game-script multipliers (the leader runs more, the trailer throws more). They are not fitted to data, so read them as directional.
  • Scenario legs are conditional bets: they only make sense if you think that script is likelier than the market says. Unconditionally they are no better than the market price; the measured edges (Browns ML at NoVig) are on the main analysis.
  • Same number on opposite teams: Denzel Boston and Chris Olave both wear #12.
  • Prices as of about 8:45 AM PT, Nov 8. Efficiency numbers cover games through last week.
