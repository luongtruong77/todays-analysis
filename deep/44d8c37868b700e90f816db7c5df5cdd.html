Method & caveats

  • St. Brown (#14) is active but played through a rib issue this week; his usage is the first thing to watch early.
  • Scenario probabilities come from the market (Pinnacle no-vig ML and spread where quoted, margin σ = 13.5), not the app's models, which trail the closing line.
  • Player projections start from each prop's market median and apply rule-of-thumb game-script multipliers (the leader runs more, the trailer throws more). They are not fitted to data, so read them as directional.
  • Scenario legs are conditional bets: they only make sense if you think that script is likelier than the market says. Unconditionally they are no better than the market price; the measured edges (Dolphins +6.5 at NoVig) are on the main analysis.
  • Same number on opposite teams: Jahmyr Gibbs and Ollie Gordon II both wear #0; Isaac TeSlaa and Chris Bell both wear #18.
  • Prices as of about 8:45 AM PT, Nov 8. Efficiency numbers cover games through last week.
