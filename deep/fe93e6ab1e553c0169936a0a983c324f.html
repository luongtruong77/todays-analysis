If the Eagles dominate

Method & caveats

  • Milton and McCarthy have small or no samples with these offenses. Barkley's season averages come from a heavily shared backfield.
  • Scenario probabilities come from the market (Pinnacle no-vig ML and spread where quoted, margin σ = 13.5), not the app's models, which trail the closing line.
  • Player projections start from each prop's market median and apply rule-of-thumb game-script multipliers (the leader runs more, the trailer throws more). They are not fitted to data, so read them as directional.
  • Scenario legs are conditional bets: they only make sense if you think that script is likelier than the market says. Unconditionally they are no better than the market price; the measured edges (Devin Singletary Over 2.5 receptions) are on the main analysis.
  • Same number on opposite teams: Isaiah Likely and Makai Lemon both wear #9; Devin Singletary and Saquon Barkley both wear #26; Dallas Goedert and Malachi Fields both wear #88.
  • Prices as of about 8:45 AM PT, Nov 8. Efficiency numbers cover games through last week.
